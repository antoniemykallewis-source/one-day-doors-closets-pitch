What we found / 1 of 3

Local details.One clear answer.

01

The map names the wrong city.

The embedded map says Lincoln, CA 95648. The address beside it says Rocklin, CA 95765.

Our fix. Use the verified Google business pin, then test the map and directions link on a phone.

Observed page mismatch. The separate directions link opens the correct business profile.

02

Saturday hours do not match.

Your site says 10 am–4 pm, by appointment. Google says 9 am–5 pm.

Our fix. Confirm the real Saturday hours and appointment rule. Show the same details everywhere.

We did not call or visit. This is a mismatch, not a claim about the true opening time.

What we found / 2 of 3

Faster pages.A more local story.

03

A slow-phone test took 17 seconds.

Google’s mobile lab test took 17.0 seconds to show the largest page element. Its speed score was 54/100.

Our fix. Lighten the first screen’s images and scripts. Retest on a slow connection.

One simulated test, not every visitor. Site-wide real-user speed data passed Google’s checks.

04

City pages repeat the same story.

The Rocklin and Sacramento pages use the same main service copy with city names swapped.

Our fix. Give each city real local projects, photos and helpful answers. Link to the local team.

Observed content pattern. We did not measure a search penalty or lost rankings.

What we found / 3 of 3

The next step.A fresh reason to act.

05

The form leaves “what next?” open.

The consultation form asks for contact and project details, but gives no clear staff reply time or time-slot choice.

Our fix. Add a realistic reply window and explain the next step. Only promise what the team can keep.

We inspected the form without submitting. Actual response speed is unknown.

06

Several ads share one sale hook.

Several active Sacramento ads start with the same Fall sale message. Other ads explain the one-day process.

Our fix. Test a local homeowner story beside the sale ads. Follow one real room from before to after.

Creative test idea, not proof the current ads are weak. Spend and results were unavailable.
